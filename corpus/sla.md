# Service level agreement

## Uptime commitment

The monthly uptime commitment is 99.9 percent on the Growth plan and 99.95
percent on the Scale plan. The Starter plan carries no uptime commitment.

## Credits

If uptime falls below the commitment, the account receives a credit. Between
99.0 and the commitment the credit is 10 percent of the monthly fee. Below
99.0 percent it is 25 percent. Below 95.0 percent it is 50 percent.

## Claiming

A credit must be claimed within 30 days of the end of the affected month.
Credits are applied to a future invoice and are never paid out in cash.

## Exclusions

Scheduled maintenance is excluded, provided it was announced at least 72 hours
in advance. Maintenance windows are limited to four hours per month.

## Support response

Growth accounts get a first response within 8 working hours. Scale accounts
get a first response within 2 hours, 24 hours a day.
