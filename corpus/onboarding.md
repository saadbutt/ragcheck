# Getting started

## Creating a workspace

Sign up, confirm your email, then choose a region. The region choice is
permanent, so pick the one closest to the majority of your users.

## Inviting people

Invitations expire after 7 days. An expired invitation can be resent from the
members page. Invitations do not consume a seat until they are accepted.

## First integration

Create a service account, generate a token, and call the health endpoint to
confirm connectivity. The health endpoint does not count against your rate
limit or your call allowance.

## Sandbox

Every workspace gets a sandbox environment with its own token. Sandbox calls
are free and are capped at 1,000 per day. Sandbox data is wiped every Sunday
at 00:00 UTC.
