# Billing and invoicing

Halden bills monthly in arrears. Invoices are generated on the first working
day of the month and are due 14 days after issue.

## Plans

The Starter plan is 49 USD per month and includes 50,000 API calls. The Growth
plan is 199 USD per month and includes 500,000 API calls. The Scale plan is
quoted individually and starts at 1,200 USD per month.

## Overage

Calls beyond the plan allowance are billed at 0.0004 USD per call on Starter
and 0.00025 USD per call on Growth. Overage is calculated per calendar month
and never carries forward to the next period.

## Changing plans

An upgrade takes effect immediately and the first invoice is prorated to the
day. A downgrade takes effect at the start of the next billing period, so you
keep the higher allowance until the period ends.

## Failed payments

A failed charge is retried after 3 days, then after a further 7 days. The
account moves to read only after the second failure. Data is retained for 90
days after an account goes read only.
